Writing this in the last month of the year feels a bit like a 2025 recap as well as what I'm doing now 😅 But, here's what's up.

## Work

I work as a Frontend Engineer at Adyen: [video update](https://youtu.be/KGDFnxNl5M8). Previously I worked as an Internal Developer Advocate. As the advocacy role evolved, it came to a point where I didn't get to do much engineering anymore, and I greatly missed that. I also wanted to grow more in engineering skills and I thought it the most effective that I just don't do that on the side, but in an engineering-focused team.

Thanks to all the support I got from my previous team, I got involved in interviews, and I'm now part of one of the frontend teams.

As of the time of writing, it's been 3 months in this team, and it's been fun. It's very interesting to see how frontend engineering is used in the context of Adyen. I'm also exposed to more engineering concepts. This journey has fueled my passion in sharing and teaching more engineering on my [coding channel](https://www.youtube.com/@deeecode).

## Content

The last few months has been very slow for me on content. At the beginning of the year, I lost a lot of motivation and I struggled mentally for several reasons. Coming back to the engineering field as motivated me a bit to continue making videos, but my life has also gotten more busy in different ways that I struggle to find a balance.

I gave up on creating courses. Courses are really time consuming and I haven't hacked my time management well enough to make it work. Last year I had some web development courses. While I had two success projects like [simpleregex.dev](https://simpleregex.dev/) and [simplified TypeScript](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLLdz3KlabJv10PbEcjYU5sgEwLzjrL4Ol), I also had unfinished projects like [simplereact.dev](https://simplereact.dev/), [simplegit.dev](https://simplegit.dev/) and then a failed project like [simple JavaScript](https://deeecode.com/courses/javascript).

I'm especially disappointed in myself for the `simplereact.dev` course because it had a lot of good potential. I think my teaching was really good, as validated by positive comments from people I don't even know. A couple of comments looked like this:

> I'm currently trying to learn React, and this course is so easy to understand with very good examples. Thank you very much

Whenever I get comments like this, somewhere in my head I say "I'm glad you enjoy the course but I'm really really sorry I didn't get to finish it".

Well I've discovered that creating one-off videos is my strongest suit. Maybe in the future I can revisit courses again or hack a way to create them in the chaos of my life currently.
